Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in Berkeley

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds
  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • Rainwater getting inside through a roof or wall damaged by a storm
  • Dirty water rising out of floor drains or sewer lines into rooms you use
  • Ground so saturated that puddles sit against the foundation walls
  • A layer of silt, dirt or debris on the floor after water recedes

What a Professional Brings to Flood Cleanup

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. This scientific approach to drying is the only reliable way to prevent secondary damage that can cost thousands more to fix.

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls
  • In areas of the property that tend to flood, raise electrical systems and appliances off the floor
  • Consider flood insurance even if you are not in a designated flood zone
  • Extend your downspouts to carry rain from the roof out past the foundation
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
